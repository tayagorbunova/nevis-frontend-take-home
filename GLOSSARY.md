# Clients dashboard

A screen in Nevis's app where advisors and their managers see how many clients they have each month, and how those clients arrived.

## Language

### People and organisation

**Company**:
The advisory firm that uses Nevis. It is the top of the hierarchy and is made up of branches.
_Avoid_: Firm, organisation, RIA

**Branch**:
An office of the company. It groups advisors.
_Avoid_: Office, location

**Advisor**:
A professional who looks after their own list of clients. Belongs to a branch.
_Avoid_: Employee, Adviser

**Manager**:
A person who runs a branch or the whole company.
_Avoid_: Admin, supervisor

**Client**:
A person or family who pays an advisor to manage their money.
_Avoid_: Customer, household

### Measuring the book

**Book of business**:
The clients an advisor looks after. A branch's or the company's book is the combined books of its advisors.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, AUM

**Client count**:
The number of clients a company, branch, advisor or acquisition channel has in a given month. It is the only measure on this dashboard.
_Avoid_: Value, figure

**Breakdown**:
A client count split into the client counts one level down: the company's by branch, a branch's by advisor, an advisor's by acquisition channel.
_Avoid_: Split, drill-down

**Acquisition channel** (short: **Channel**):
One of three groups a client count is split into, by how the clients arrived: Existing clients, New organic or New paid.
_Avoid_: Attribute, source

**Existing clients**:
Clients who were already in the book the month before.

**New organic**:
New clients who arrived without paid advertising, for example through a recommendation.

**New paid**:
New clients who arrived through paid advertising.
